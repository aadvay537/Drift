How Drift works

The AI reads. Simple, published maths decides. You see everything before it's sent.

  1. Your file stays here

    It's opened inside this page. It's never uploaded to us.

    On your device

  2. Names come out

    Creator names and @handles are removed before anything leaves.

    On your device

  3. An AI tags each title

    Only cleaned titles go out, to be tagged with a topic and how emotionally charged they are. Then they're deleted.

    Titles only

  4. Fixed rules decide

    Published thresholds compare your recent weeks with your own earlier weeks. Same file, same answer, every time.

    On your device

  5. A calm report

    One AI writes it and a second checks the tone: no scare words, no blame, no comparing you to anyone.

    Summary only

What Drift looks for

Narrowing

Fewer topics are taking up more of your time. Shown when your range of topics shrinks by more than 15%.

Escalation

What you watch is getting more emotionally charged. Shown when the average rises by more than 10%.

Engagement drift

Shorter videos, more often. Shown when videos get 20% shorter and you open the app 25% more often.

Instagram and TikTok downloads don't include video titles, so for those Drift looks at your rhythm — how often and when you watch — and, for Instagram, which creators fill your feed.
