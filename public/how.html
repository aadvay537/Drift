How Drift works

The AI reads. Simple, published rules decide.

  1. Add your history

    Screenshots or your app's download.

  2. Names come out

    Removed on your device.

  3. AI tags topics

    Titles only, then deleted.

  4. Rules decide

    Recent weeks vs your earlier weeks.

  5. Report + plan

    Calm, checked, practical.

What Drift looks for

Narrowing

Fewer topics take up more of your time. Shown when your range of topics shrinks by more than 15%.

Escalation

What you watch gets more emotionally charged. Shown when the average rises by more than 10%.

Engagement drift

Shorter videos, more often. Shown when videos get 20% shorter and you open the app 25% more often.

The Drift Score

0–100: how far your recent weeks moved from your own earlier weeks. Not good or bad — just how big the change is.

Instagram and TikTok

With screenshots, the AI recognises the topics from the thumbnails. With the app's download, Drift shows your rhythm — when and how often — because downloads have no titles.
