Narrowing

Fewer topics are taking up more of your time. Fires when your distinct topics shrink by more than 15%.

Escalation

What you watch is getting more emotionally charged. Fires when average intensity rises more than 10%.

Engagement drift

Shorter videos, more often. Fires when videos get 20% shorter and sessions 25% more frequent.

Private by design, not by promise

Your history file is opened inside this page and never uploaded. When you watch, how long, and who you watch are worked out on your own device. Only cleaned-up titles reach the AI, which deletes them and never trains on them. For Instagram and TikTok, nothing from your file leaves at all. All the code is public, so anyone can check.

Questions

Is the Drift Score good or bad?

Neither. It's how far your recent weeks moved from your own earlier weeks, from 0 to 100. A high number just means a bigger change.

Why does Instagram or TikTok show less than YouTube?

Their downloads don't include video titles, so there's nothing to read for topics. Drift still shows your rhythm — when and how often you watch — and, for Instagram, which creators take up your feed and what interests Instagram has guessed for you.

How long does my data download take?

Google Takeout with only YouTube history is usually ready in minutes. Instagram and TikTok can take from a few minutes to a couple of days — they email you when it's ready.

Does Drift save anything?

Only if you press "Save to this device". That keeps a small summary (your score and drift type — no titles) in your browser so you can compare runs later. Clear it any time.

Can I trust the result?

Drift shows patterns, not proof. If there isn't enough history it says so instead of guessing, and every report shows how confident it is.
