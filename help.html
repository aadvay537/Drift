Help

Can't find your answer? Press Ask Drift in the bottom corner and type your question.

Where do I get my history file?

On the Get your report page, pick your app. Drift shows you exactly where to click inside that app to download your own history.

How long does the download take?

Google Takeout with only YouTube history is usually ready within minutes. Instagram and TikTok can take from a few minutes to a couple of days — they email you when it's ready.

Do I need to unzip the file?

No. You can add the whole .zip file as it is.

Is the Drift Score good or bad?

Neither. It's how far your recent weeks moved from your own earlier weeks, from 0 to 100. A higher number just means a bigger change.

Why does Instagram or TikTok show less than YouTube?

Their downloads don't include video titles, so there's nothing to read for topics. Drift still shows how often and when you watch, and for Instagram, which creators fill your feed.

What if the page says "Waking the AI"?

The AI server rests when nobody is using it. It wakes up while you get your file. If it's slow, Drift finishes your report on your own device instead.

Can I trust the result?

Drift shows patterns, not proof. If there isn't enough history it says so instead of guessing, and every report shows how confident it is.

Does Drift save anything?

Only what you choose. "Save to this device" keeps a tiny summary in your own browser so you can compare later. Sharing an anonymous summary is optional and asked at the start.
