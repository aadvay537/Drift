Private by design, not by promise

Your history file is opened inside this page and is never uploaded. When you watch, how long, and who you watch are all worked out on your own device.

For YouTube and Reddit, only cleaned-up video titles go to the AI, to be tagged with a topic. The AI service deletes them and never trains on them. For Instagram and TikTok, nothing from your file is sent at all.

The optional anonymous summary

Before you start, Drift asks whether you're happy to share an anonymous summary of your result to help improve it. It's completely optional, and saying no changes nothing about your report.

If you say yes, Drift keeps your drift type, score, weekly totals, topic percentages, your answer to "Did this feel accurate?" and your age group — nothing else. If you're 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must agree first. Nothing is shared for anyone under 13, or for example reports.

After sharing, a "Remove my summary" button appears on your report, and it's remembered on your device so you can remove it later.
