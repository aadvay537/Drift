Privacy

  • Your history file is never uploaded
  • Names, dates and handles stay on your device
  • Nothing is shared unless you say yes
  • Never compares you to anyone
What goes to the AI?

For YouTube and Reddit: cleaned video titles, to tag topics — then deleted. For Instagram and TikTok screenshots: the screenshots, to recognise topics — then deleted. You always see what's sent first. The AI service doesn't train on it.

The optional anonymous summary

Asked at the start. If you say yes, Drift keeps your result type, score, weekly totals, topic percentages, your "was it accurate?" answer and age group — nothing else. Under-13s and example reports never share. 13–17s need a parent's OK.

Removing your summary

A "Remove my summary" button appears on your report. It's remembered on your device so you can remove it later.

Ask Drift chats

Saved only in your browser. Questions go to the AI to be answered, with only your report's summary numbers — never your history.

Daily limit

Each person gets 4 AI uses a day, counted with a random ID in your browser. Only the count is stored.
